write 2 1 0

STATEMENT
Win by forming a line or a 2x2 square of 4 pieces sharing a characteristic!

INPUT
myId: Your player ID (0 or 1).
pieceToPlace: The ID of the piece you must place (from 0 to 31).
cell0: Row values (-1 for empty, -2 for corners, 0-31 for piece ID).
N: Number of available pieces.
availablePiece: ID of an available piece.

OUTPUT
x y id - Coordinates to place your piece (x y) and the ID of the piece for your opponent (id).
